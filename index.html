Building a better metadata schema

A photo library should work like a well-stocked pantry, where anyone finds what they need by reading the labels. At Discover Durham, our labels fell short. Staff asked my creative team almost every week to track down photos already in the library or to confirm whether they had the rights to use them. With zero budget and no developers, I interviewed our users and asset managers, mapped three broken user flows, and rebuilt the metadata schema and cataloging workflow in Photoshelter and Lightroom Classic.

On the user side, I added a filterable usage-rights field, richer captions and category keywords, and individual logins. On the cataloging side, a custom Lightroom metadata panel and a preset for each photographer cut hand-entered fields from 16 to 7. Rights questions dropped from weekly to once every couple of months, and the streamlined workflow let our team catalog nearly 9,000 new photos in 18 months.
